College Overview  
CVR College of Engineering (CVRCE) is a private autonomous engineering college near Ibrahimpatnam, Hyderabad, affiliated to JNTU Hyderabad. It is known for strong placements and is managed by the Cherabuddi Education Society. The Society seeks to harness the technical expertise and financial support of NRIs to make CVR College of Engineering a pre-eminent technical institution.

Academic Regulations  
A minimum of 75% attendance is required to appear for end-semester examinations. Attendance can be condoned down to 65% on genuine grounds with a fee, and below 65% leads to detention and re-registration of that semester when offered. Medical leave can be applied for by submitting valid documents to the academic office.  
B.Tech is a 4-year (8-semester) program, and each semester is 22 weeks with at least 90 instructional days. Admission is through TS EAMCET or other university/government-approved merit criteria.  
Each course carries 100 marks: 40 internal (CIE) and 60 external (SEE). A 10-point absolute grading scale is used: S, A+, A, B+, B, C, P, F, and Ab. SGPA and CGPA are computed over registered credits, and a minimum CGPA of 5.0 is required to be considered passed in the semester/program.  
A student is promoted to the next academic year only if the minimum prescribed credits are secured in preceding semesters as per promotion rules. Supplementary examinations are conducted for failed theory and laboratory courses, and the better of the two performances is retained wherever applicable.  
Malpractice in examinations results in cancellation of performance in that course and may attract additional disciplinary action as per regulations. Students may apply for revaluation or recounting within the notified time by paying the prescribed fee.  
Industry-oriented mini projects must be completed as per the academic calendar and carry credits defined in the curriculum. Final-year project work is evaluated through continuous assessment and a final viva-voce by internal and external examiners.  
A break in study is permitted only with prior approval of the college academic council, and rejoining is under regulations in force at the time of readmission. To be eligible for the B.Tech degree, a student must earn all prescribed credits, clear all courses, and satisfy minimum CGPA requirements as per regulations.  
A student must register for all courses offered in a semester, and withdrawal is permitted only within notified timelines with academic council approval. Makeup tests are provided only for genuine medical or emergency reasons with supporting documents approved by the department.  
For theory courses, a student is declared passed only by securing minimum required marks separately in internal assessment and semester-end examinations. Backlog courses must be cleared through supplementary examinations, and credits are awarded only after passing all course components. Improvement of internal assessment marks may be permitted in the subsequent offering of the course as per academic guidelines.  
Students must strictly follow examination rules, and possession of unauthorized material or unfair means leads to exam cancellation and further disciplinary action. SGPA is computed at the end of each semester, and CGPA is computed cumulatively over all completed semesters. A student completes the program only after successfully clearing all registered courses, laboratories, and project components.  
Break of study due to medical or personal reasons must be formally requested and approved, and readmitted students follow regulations in force at that time. Students must maintain academic integrity in assignments, laboratory records, project reports, and publications.  
For laboratory courses, attendance, continuous evaluation, and final practical examination performance together determine the final grade. If a student is detained for attendance shortage, all internal marks of that semester are cancelled and the semester must be repeated.  
Students must follow the academic calendar for registrations, fee payments, internal assessments, and exam form submissions.  
For students promoted to B.Tech IV Year in 2025-26 under Convener/Management Quota, the total tuition fee is Rs. 1,56,385 for CIVIL, EEE, and MECH, and Rs. 1,53,385 for CSE (CS), CSE (AI & ML), and CSE (DS). Hostel Facilities  
The college provides on-campus hostel accommodation as a major student support service. Hostel facilities were established in the academic year 2009-10 to support a residential campus environment. The college believes that a residential campus offers a superior environment for academic learning.  
Hostels are designed to provide a comfortable and supportive living environment. Students residing in hostels have access to a wide range of activities, facilities, and support services.  
The college has two boys’ hostels and two girls’ hostels on campus. The combined accommodation capacity is 1,250 students for the academic year 2025-26.  
The annual hostel fee for boys and girls is Rs. 1,60,000 for air-conditioned rooms and Rs. 1,35,000 for non-air-conditioned rooms in 2025-26. A refundable caution deposit of Rs. 10,000 is collected at hostel admission.

Infrastructure and Campus Facilities  
The college has a built-up area of over 36,000 sqm on a 33-acre campus with verdant surroundings.  
There are 119 laboratories across departments, including computer, electronics, mechanical, and other departmental labs. The college has exactly 31 computer laboratories dedicated to computing and IT-related activities.  
Computer labs are equipped with Lenovo, Dell, and Apple servers and systems. The campus provides high-speed internet of approximately 2 Gbps with campus-wide Wi-Fi.  
The quality of computing and network infrastructure enables the college to regularly host online recruitment examinations for various companies. The college was the only institution in the Telangana region selected to host the inaugural Smart India Hackathon.  
All faculty members are provided personal computers or laptops with high-speed internet access. Professors are provided high-end laptops, laser printers, and scanners for academic use. Students are provided appropriate internet access for learning and academic activities.  
Computing equipment is regularly upgraded, and previously used equipment is donated to local schools.  
The Student Centre is an independent four-story building housing facilities such as a canteen, gym, indoor sports facilities, and placement office.  
The college health centre provides essential medical services for students and staff. It has a dedicated doctor and a small team of nurses. Basic inpatient facilities with a few beds are available for short-term care. The health centre is equipped with an ambulance, and patients can be transported to nearby hospitals when required.

Research and Innovation  
The college has a well-defined research policy covering research facilities and incentives. It provides strong research infrastructure, including high-configuration computers and equipment for project design and development.  
Seed money is provided to support research and innovation. The college has established an EDC and NewGen Incubation Center to promote entrepreneurship and innovation.  
Specialized research centers include a UAV Design Center and centers for Advanced Antenna Design and Nano Technology Materials Research with advanced software and equipment.  
The college promotes patent filing through financial support and incentives. It provides paid study leave of six months on three occasions during the Ph.D. program. Ph.D. scholars receive a research allowance of Rs. 60,000 and an incentive of Rs. 25,000 at three stages of the Ph.D.  
Digital library and e-resource access are provided to students and faculty to support research.  
The CVR Journal of Science and Technology started in 2011. Faculty are encouraged to publish in standard national and international journals. The institution ensures implementation of a research code of ethics.  
More than 2,000 research papers have been published in SCOPUS and Web of Science indexed journals. Twenty-two professors have been identified as research guides and supervisors.  
The college regularly organizes workshops and seminars on research methodology, intellectual property rights, entrepreneurship, and skill development.

Data Science Department
The Department of Computer Science and Engineering (Data Science) was established in 2020 to equip students with cutting-edge knowledge and skills in the rapidly evolving fields of Data Science (DS), Big Data analytics, Advanced Databases and  Cognitive Analytics.
CSE (Data Science) is an upcoming, exponentially growing field which consists of a set of tools and techniques used to extract useful information from data. There is a rising demand for undergraduates in Data Science in the Global market over the last few years. B.Tech. CSE (Data Science) programme started from the academic year 2020 – 21 with a vision to create globally competent students with expertise in Data analytic skills & emerging areas of data science applications. B.Tech in CSE (Data Science) has been specifically designed to educate undergraduate students for a prospective career in upcoming data science industry. With analysis and statistical approach, data science is one of the fastest growing career opportunities in the country. The program encompass data science as an interdisciplinary, problem-solving-oriented subject that learns to apply scientific techniques to practical problems.
B.Tech in CSE (DS)  is best suited for students who are seeking to build a career in field of data science. The curriculum include courses on programming, algorithms, data structures, computer networks, database management, and operating systems. In addition, the program educates them on emerging areas of Data Science topics such as Machine Learning, Statistics, Optimization, Big Data Analytics, NoSQL Databases, Real time Analytics and many more. This helps the students to stand apart in the fellow graduates and grow careers in the upcoming technological era.
Graduates in CSE (DS) have career prospects in a variety of industries such as Retail, Finance, E-commerce, Healthcare, IT services with job roles defined as Data Scientist, Data Analyst, Business Analyst, Data Engineer and many more.
